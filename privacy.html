Privacy Policy

Velora for iPhone · Effective 17 October 2026
VeloraTerms of Use

Velora is built so that your videos never leave your device. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information. In short: Velora does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data.

Who we are

Velora is developed by Bruno Petković ("we", "us"), Croatia. You can contact us at bruno.petkovic.business@gmail.com.

Your videos and photos

When you choose a video, Velora reads it from your photo library or the Files app and processes it entirely on your iPhone. Videos, frames, audio and the location or date stored in them are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Velora keeps temporary working copies on your device and removes them automatically. Exported videos are saved only where you choose (Photos or Files). Velora asks for permission to add videos to your photo library only so it can save your exports.

On-device AI

Frame interpolation and AI upscaling use processing built into iOS. When you enable AI upscaling for the first time, iOS may download Apple's model to your device. That download is handled by Apple; Velora does not send your content anywhere as part of it.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. Velora only learns from Apple whether Velora Pro is active on your Apple Account so it can unlock features. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Information stored on your device

To provide one free video, Velora stores a small counter in your device's Keychain. It also stores your settings (such as theme and export format) and a list of your recent exports in the app's local storage. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Velora contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software, and does not track you across apps or websites. Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous App Store statistics and crash reports if you have chosen to share them with developers in your device settings.

Children

Velora does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove all of Velora's on-device data by deleting the app (the free-video counter in the Keychain may remain until you reset your device). If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have rights under the GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
